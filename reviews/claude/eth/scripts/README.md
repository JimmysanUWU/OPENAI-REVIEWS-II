# Exact-arithmetic checks

The scripts use Python 3.10+ and the standard library only (`fractions`, `itertools`). Each is deterministic and writes JSON to `../results/`.

| Script | Checks | Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| `eth_core.py` | Library: auxiliary array $\mathcal B$, $W_0$, $V_0$, $f_{\mathcal I}$, $M_0$, $C^{\mathcal B}$, rows $P_u(T)$ (Sections 4.1–4.3), random bounded-occurrence CNFs, brute-force counts. | — |
| `verify_replacement_forms.py` | Lemmas 4.1–4.3 and Remark 4.4, for $(r,Q,R)\in\{(2,2,2),(2,3,2),(2,3,3),(2,4,2),(2,4,3),(3,2,2),(3,3,2)\}$. Exact rank of $I-M_0$. Negative controls ($f\equiv1$; wrong interval). | ~6 s |
| `verify_counting_pipeline.py` | The full algorithm of §3–§4 against brute force on 17 CNFs, plus a symbolic $H(T)$ and a deleted-measurement negative control. | ~1 min |
| `verify_parity_lift.py` | Lemmas 5.1 and 5.2 (congruences, decomposition, the degree $m+2(\ell-1)$) and the coefficient sizes of Lemma B.1. | <1 s |
| `verify_parameters.py` | (2.19) and Lemma 4.8: least $\lambda=\lceil\log_2n\rceil$ at which all hypotheses hold and at which the bound beats $2^n$. | <1 s |
| `verify_appendix_d.py` | Lemma D.4, identity (D.10), Lemma D.5 (with a precision negative control), expansion (D.12). | ~2 s |
| `verify_distilled_swap_formula.py` | Theorem B of `05_DISTILLATION.md` (closed form), and Proposition B″ (symmetric optimality). | ~1 s |
| `verify_padic_truncation.py` | TOOLKIT E2: the $p$-adic cross-equation truncation for $p\in\{2,3,5,7\}$, $\ell\le6$ (congruences, decomposition, truncation, degree bound). | <1 s |
| `verify_gapless_chain.py` | [08](../08_GAPLESS_PROOF.md), every lemma: sparsification Lemmas 2.1–2.3 and Prop. 2.4 at every node of 32 trees, plus the partition; OR gates and Tovey cycles on 40 formulas; the full counting algorithm mod $p^\mu$, with closed-form rows, derandomized schedules and 2-group measurement rows, on 16 instances against brute force (copies $h\le3$), with a negative control; parameter inequalities (P1)–(P8) and the exponent bound; assembly constants. | ~30 s |
| `verify_sharp_route.py` | [09](../09_GAPLESS_SHARP_EXPONENT.md): the closed-form $h$-adic digits of the parity lift (Lemma 2.1) against repeated division for $\ell\le128$, with a negative control; counting common zeros of sparse $\mathbb F_2$ systems through the $V$-truncated algebra mod $p^\mu$ on 8 instances against brute force, with a negative control (Theorem 3.1); Tanner-code toys and the constants of Lemma 4.1; greedy rows by exact counts (Lemma 4.2). | ~11 s |
| `verify_tensor_contraction.py` | [10](../10_GAPLESS_TENSOR_CONTRACTION.md), App. D end to end. The child identity (D.16) on 66 packed networks, with brute-force children, two negative controls, and the tile expansion mod $2^{40}$. One recursion level at the honest precision: Lagrange over $\mathbb Z_{(2)}[U]/(f)$ plus tapered recovery, exact in 8/8 and failing in 8/8 without the extra precision. Also the mode-tuple count, Lemma D.2's incidence networks, the clause network, and the parameter thresholds. | ~45 s |
| `verify_fp_counting.py` | [12](../12_FP_COUNTING.md). The $p$-adic lift: E2 digits and the closed-form idempotent, for $p=2,3,5,7$ and $\ell\le8$, with a negative control. The full counting pipeline over $\mathbb F_3$, $\mathbb F_5$, $\mathbb F_7$ (bit encoding, validity factors, Part I deformation, CRT), exact in 8/8 cases. Negative controls: without validity factors (8/8 wrong) and with too few jets (4/4 wrong). | ~7 s |
| `verify_unified_deformation.py` | [13](../13_UNIFIED_DEFORMATION.md): the filtered node lemma, targeted (p-adic) and Boolean (z-adic), on 27 random readout systems with measurements, plus witnesses and negative controls; Hermite recovery with valuation loss (unit nodes: zero loss; residue systems: the loss formula $D_i\Delta(i)$, recovery at the rule's precision, one bit less fails); correction counts against exact counts; the profile lemma, the exact optimal depth profile and the paper's taper ($\to6M/a$); the generic evaluators on genuine Part I deformations and Part III packed networks. | ~30 s |
| `verify_design_floor.py` | [14](../14_DESIGN_FLOOR.md): Smolensky's bound by exact rational elimination (180 random sets of $N_D+1$ points; control in degree $D+2$); the biased-measure bound by restriction on window and greedy non-symmetric designs, with the coupling identity exact and a degree-$(D+2)$ control; the explicit forms of Lemma 2.3; the floor for all graded designs over $\log_2n=2^{10..300}$; ungraded against single-polynomial designs on central windows (`--full` adds $(8,4)$). | ~70 s |
| `verify_cost_floor.py` | TOOLKIT E4: minimizes the method's three costs over integer parameters with a rigorous window bound; compares with $(\log\log n)^2/\sqrt{\log n}$. | ~2 min |

Run them from this directory, for example `python3 verify_counting_pipeline.py`.
