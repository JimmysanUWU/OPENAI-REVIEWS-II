# Checks for the Kakeya / local smoothing paper

Requirements: Python 3.10+, `numpy`, `sympy`, `scipy`. Each script is deterministic and writes JSON to `../results/`.

| Script | Checks | Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| `verify_wave_current.py` | The plane-wave phase in null coordinates (2.1); the symbol (2.2) and $\mathscr Pf_{\rm sys}=0$; the Gaussian identity (2.4); **Lemma 2.1** (PSD and divergence-free lifted tensor, $n=2,3$, random superpositions) with a negative control; the holomorphic flux map (2.9)–(2.13). | ~1 min |
| `verify_quaternion_calculus.py` | **Lemma 3.1** (Hessian of rank-one forms, trace, product rule, $H(\sum\lvert F\rvert^2)=4J^*J$); **Lemma 3.2** (Moore determinant identities (3.6)–(3.8), concavity); **Lemma 3.3** (cofactor divergence, exact symbolic, 2/3/4 slots) with a negative control; **Lemma 4.3** segment identity and weights. | ~10 s |
| `verify_propagation_and_exponents.py` | **Lemma 6.1** radial comparison (Gronwall constant, $h$ down to $10^{-6}$); **Remark 6.2** sharpness example (exact Laplacian); exponent bookkeeping for $p_c$, (7.3), Prop 8.1, Lemma 9.4, Kakeya log powers, Stein threshold, and the Lean doc's power 66. | ~1 s |
| `verify_wave_current_target.py` | [08](../08_GAPLESS_PROOF.md) Prop. 3.3 (paper Prop. 2.2), checked **directly in the target coordinates**, independently of the push-forward argument. For random superpositions with $n=2,3$: the real divergence of $(\chi\chi^*)^{\mathbb R}$ vanishes, with central differences in 60-digit arithmetic (relative size $\sim10^{-39}$); $(\partial\Phi)\chi=0$; and $A^0_{TT}=\lvert\mathsf S_0u\rvert^2+\lvert\mathsf S_1u\rvert^2$. Two negative controls (no rotation $\iota_e$; no volume factor) fail at order one. | ~2 s |
| `verify_remaining_claims.py` | [10](../10_GAPLESS_REMAINING.md). It checks:<br>• App. C's tube arrangement (exact interval unions, $k\le16$);<br>• the Nikodym projective map (symbolic);<br>• §12's parabolic rescaling powers and the Fresnel identity;<br>• the cone's slicing exponents;<br>• Example E.2 and formula (E.2) on random pencils;<br>• App. D's quaternionic identities (completed square, rank-one Moore expansion). | ~35 s |
| `verify_positivity_engine.py` | [11](../11_FRAMEWORK_POSITIVITY_ENGINE.md), in 60-digit arithmetic, for the arrow pencils ($n=2,3,4$), the cubic of Example E.2 and a perturbed arrow pencil:<br>• **pencil geometry:** Hellmann–Feynman, the curvature formula, $Dv=S_\perp^{-1}L$, $dB(0)=2L^{\mathsf T}$, stationarity, the characteristic conormal; the cubic's transverse Hessian $\mathrm{diag}(1,\frac23)$;<br>• **the pencil current, directly in target coordinates:** conservation ($\sim10^{-38}$ relative), tangency, $J=\lvert R\rvert^{2n}j(W)$, the Gaussian and row identities; negative controls (wrong conjugation, no volume factor) fail;<br>• **a middle eigenvalue:** conserved, but its window exceeds $1$;<br>• **the wave is the arrow pencil** (exact, sympy). | ~10 s |
| `verify_slot_calculus.py` | [12](../12_QUATERNIONIC_SLOT_CALCULUS.md):<br>• cofactor divergence for **arbitrary quaternion frames** (exact, five frames of sizes 1–5, including dependent ones; two negative controls);<br>• frame reduction to the 4-slot calculus;<br>• the real and complex cases;<br>• the 3-slot real ⊕ complex decomposition;<br>• ellipticity iff independent frames;<br>• Euler's identity and $C(A)>0$. | ~12 s |
| `verify_capacity_dimension.py` | TOOLKIT K4: superharmonic sharpness examples in $\mathbb R_T\times\mathbb R^k_\tau$, $k=1,\dots,5$; only $k=2$ decays logarithmically. Exact Laplacian, 50-digit nonnegativity, fitted exponents. | ~7 s |
